Workflow Transparency

How CivicConnect Works

A seamless 4-step municipal accountability flow from reporting to confirmed resolution

1

Report Issue

Capture a photo or video with geotagged EXIF data. Pin the precise GPS coordinates on the map and choose the problem category.

2

Smart Routing

The platform automatically identifies the assigned Ward Nagarsevak and routes the complaint directly to the concerned municipal department (PWD, Water, Electrical).

3

Live Tracking

Track real-time resolution progress with your unique Ticket ID. Receive SMS, WhatsApp, and in-app alerts as field crews are dispatched.

4

Proof & Closure

Contractors upload verified after-repair photo proof. Citizens confirm satisfactory resolution or trigger automated MLA escalation.

Platform Capabilities

Built for Citizens and Municipal Leaders

Modern technology ensuring zero lost complaints, transparent tracking, and verified resolutions

GPS & Ward Auto-Detection

Instantly pins exact latitude and longitude, automatically determining the municipal ward, electoral boundaries, and assigned corporator without manual research.

AI Duplicate Detection

Identifies existing complaints within a 150-meter radius, allowing citizens to upvote existing tickets instead of creating duplicate backlogs.

Multi-Tier Auto-Escalation

Every complaint is systematically monitored. If unaddressed by local authorities, the system automatically escalates the issue to the constituency MLA for oversight.

Real-Time Multi-Channel Alerts

Receive automated SMS, WhatsApp, and email alerts when your complaint is verified, scheduled for field repair, and officially completed.

Public Ward Accountability Dashboard

Citizens and authorities can review ward-level resolution rates, average resolution times, and departmental responsiveness ratings—ensuring continuous municipal improvement.

Accountability Framework

Citizen → Nagarsevak → MLA Escalation Ladder

A built-in governance mechanism guaranteeing that unaddressed civic issues never get forgotten

Level 1 • Ward Jurisdiction

Citizen to Ward Nagarsevak

The citizen files the report with GPS & photo. It appears instantly on the local Ward Corporator's console for immediate triage and department routing.

Responsible: Ward Nagarsevak & Ward Office
Level 2 • Technical Dispatch

Ward Junior Engineers & PWD

Concerned municipal engineers (Roads, Water, Solid Waste) inspect the site, allocate materials, and deploy repair crews with active progress tracking.

Responsible: Municipal Executive Engineers & Contractors
Level 3 • Legislative Oversight

Auto-Escalation to MLA (Aamdar)

If the ticket is neglected or stalled by local authorities, it auto-escalates to the constituency MLA's dashboard for high-level legislative intervention.

Responsible: Member of Legislative Assembly (MLA)
